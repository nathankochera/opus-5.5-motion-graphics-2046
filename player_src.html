2046, a design brief.

A 30-second showreel about where AI and robotics could take us in twenty years. Each chapter is one objective in the brief: cities that give streets back, science that runs at the speed of software, robots on the dull, dirty and dangerous work, clean power that is cheap, and expert help for everyone.

The ending is the point. 2046 is something we design, so the brief closes on the questions it has to answer: who benefits, who decides, what stays human.

Everything is code. Each frame is drawn on a canvas, and the score is synthesized from oscillators and noise on the same timeline, so every cut lands on a downbeat. The player above renders live in your browser from the same source as the video file.
